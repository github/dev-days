# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Developers, workshop participants, and event organizers exploring hands-on GitHub Copilot learning experiences across editors, languages, and workflows.

## Product Purpose

Help visitors quickly discover the right GitHub Copilot workshop, understand its scope and prerequisites, and connect with nearby Dev Days events or reusable workshop materials.

## Brand Personality

Energetic, technical, and welcoming. The experience should make practical AI-assisted development feel approachable without losing credibility with experienced developers.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic corporate and overly formal styling that makes the event feel distant, static, or indistinguishable from a conventional documentation portal.

## Design Principles

- Make workshop choices easy to scan and compare.
- Keep the energy high without sacrificing clarity.
- Practice what the event teaches by presenting technical content confidently and accessibly.
- Welcome developers across tools, languages, and experience levels.
- Preserve GitHub and GitHub Copilot brand familiarity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, reduced-motion preferences, sufficient color contrast, semantic structure, and accessible names for icon-only controls.
