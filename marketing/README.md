# 📣 Marketing Toolkit

This marketing toolkit provides everything you need to successfully promote your **Dev Days** event. All materials are professionally designed and ready to customize with your event details.

## 💜 Series Hashtag

When posting about your Dev Days event, include our series hashtag in your post: **#DevDays**

## 🎨 Generate Event Assets

Use the event [asset generator](https://gh.io/dev-day/assets) to create your event's:
- Luma event page cover
- Speaker banner
- Social promo 

> 💡 You can also use the **[Registration Page template](draft-registration-page.md)** available in this toolkit to ensure consistent messaging between your event page and promotional assets.
